How Shaikh Capital works

  1. Every morning at 07:30 Shaikh Capital automatically checks ~1,300 stocks across the US 🇺🇸, Germany 🇩🇪 and India 🇮🇳.
  2. It hunts for chart patterns that often come before a price rise, checks the stock's trend and momentum, and rates the quality of the company (growth, profits, debt, dividends).
  3. The best candidates show up as a ranked list of setups. A = strongest. Click one to see everything about it.
  4. Each setup gives you a complete plan of three prices and a time limit: Buy above (only buy if the price rises past this — and not if it opens far above the "buy up to" price), Safety stop (sell here if it drops — this caps your loss at roughly 1% of your capital with the suggested share count), Target (where to take your profit), and the time exit (if neither stop nor target is hit, sell at the close of the last allowed trading day — the backtest numbers assume this).
  5. The colored banner shows overall market health. 🔴 Red means the whole market is falling — even good-looking stocks fail often then. Best to wait.
  6. Took a trade? Press Log this trade and enter the exact price you paid. Shaikh Capital then watches it hourly and pops up a notification when your stop or target is hit, and a reminder on the morning of the time-exit day (sell at that day's close). The 📒 Journal tab shows how you're really doing over time.
  7. Two guards come from a real lesson: at most 2 setups per sector make the active list each day (the rest are greyed out as "sector full"), because a basket of five picks that were really four financials moved as one when that sector fell — and when an open position trades at or below its safety stop, the Journal and the alert say STOP HIT — sell instead of "review", because holding past the stop once turned a planned 5% loss into 11%. The cap is max_per_sector in config.json.
